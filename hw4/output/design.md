# Campus Customs Online: Design (Problem 10)

**Concept:** a 1996 Yale homepage in the spirit of the original Space Jam site. Yale Blue and Handsome Dan stay; everything else is starfields, Netscape windows, WordArt, and cartoon pops. Styles live in `frontend/src/retro.css`; the pieces live in `frontend/src/components/Retro.tsx`.

## What changed, and why it keeps people around and buying

| Area | What changed | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| **Backdrop** | Drifting Yale-Blue **starfield** with yellow and cyan stars; a **scrolling marquee** ("NEW!!! Chat with Handsome Dan", "100% Harvard-free zone", "Beat Harvard!") that pauses on hover. | Instantly memorable and on-brand for a spirited campus shop; the marquee advertises the chat and categories without taking page space. |
| **Page frame** | Every page sits in a **Netscape Navigator window**: blue title bar, _ □ × buttons, a Location bar showing the current page, and a pulsing "N". Nav is a beveled **Win95 toolbar**. | Content lives on a light "paper" panel, so text stays readable on the busy background; the location bar shows shoppers where they are. |
| **Type and hierarchy** | **WordArt headings** (Impact, yellow-to-orange gradient, blue outline, skew); Comic Sans-style labels and buttons; Times body text; pixel-font eyebrows with blinking ★. | Three clear levels (headline, label, body) make pages easy to scan; the retro fonts carry the joke without hurting readability. |
| **Color pops** | Yellow **starburst prices**, red "BOOLA BOOLA!" corner ribbon, red/yellow **blinking pixel badges**, rubber-stamp **SOLD OUT**, and an animated **sunburst** behind the home headline. | Pops are reserved for money and urgency (price, low stock, sold out), so the eye lands on buying information first. |
| **Product presentation** | Cards have thick black outlines, hard cartoon shadows, and a **boing** wobble on hover; the price starburst **pops**. Real-data badges: **UNDER $40!** (price < $40), **HURRY! SOME SIZES ALMOST GONE!** (any size with ≤ 5 left), and a **SOLD OUT** stamp. The detail page shows the photo as a **taped polaroid** with a giant $ starburst and **beveled size buttons**. | Playful hover feedback invites browsing more items. Urgency badges are truthful and drawn from inventory, nudging purchases without fake scarcity. |
| **Find-O-Matic 3000™** | Problem 9's filters restyled as a Win95 dialog with beveled category buttons and sunken inputs. | Makes the most useful tool on the page impossible to miss. |
| **Chat feel** | Handsome Dan is now an **AOL Instant Messenger** buddy: "Instant Message with HandsomeDan96" title bar, red **HandsomeDan96:** / blue **YourName:** screen names, "HandsomeDan96 is typing…", **Send ➤**. Yellow bulldog launcher that **hops** every few seconds, with an "IM me!" bubble that peeks in and out. | A familiar, fun chat format plus a gentle bounce gets more shoppers to open the assistant, which answers size and stock questions that close sales. |

Webring lists ~~Harvard~~ "(blocked by webmaster)"; a "🚫 NO CRIMSON" badge; Dan's prompt allows good-natured ribbing only, and points Harvard questions to the real *2025 Yale vs Harvard T-shirt*. | Shareable "you have to try this" moments build loyalty and word of mouth with Yale buyers; the rivalry stays friendly, and the joke still leads to a product. |

| **Forms and 404** | Log in and Create Account are **Win95 dialogs** ("Campus Customs Online - Member Area"); errors get ⚠️; the 404 page has Dan: "Ruff! Page not found". | Familiar dialog styling makes forms obvious; errors stand out. |

## Guardrails
- **Readable first:** dark text on light panels; motion stays in the background or appears only on hover.
- **Reduced motion:** with *reduce motion* turned on, the marquee, blinking, wobble, sparkles, sunburst, and launcher hop are all disabled (CSS media query, plus the sparkles script checks it). This rule is written but couldn't be tested in this browser.
- **Phones:** checked at 375px. The window frame, filters, and chat fit with no sideways scrolling; the "IM me!" bubble and address label hide on small screens.
- **No fake data:** prices, stock badges, and the visitor count all come from the database.
